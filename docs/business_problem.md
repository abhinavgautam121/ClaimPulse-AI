# Business problem and product decisions

Denials, slow reimbursement and fragmented work queues can make it difficult for revenue-cycle teams to decide what needs review. ClaimPulse connects financial balances, denial history, age and follow-up context so a reviewer can trace a KPI to individual synthetic claims.

The executive view provides an overview; denial analysis identifies contributions to originally denied expected reimbursement; A/R and priority views expose current balances and deadlines. Payer comparisons have explicit denominators and minimum sample requirements. Underpayments rely on clearly labelled synthetic contract assumptions. Copilot answers a bounded set of operational questions with supporting tables instead of inventing SQL or figures.

No measured savings, increased collections or approval improvements are claimed. The project demonstrates an engineering and analytics approach, not an operationally validated healthcare product. It is independent and does not reproduce RapidClaims proprietary software.
